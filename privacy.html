Privacy & Cookie Policy

Last updated: June 2026

1. Introduction

Atkinson IT Ltd (“we”, “us”, “our”) is committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring your personal data is handled securely and responsibly. This combined Privacy & Cookie Policy explains:

  • What personal data we collect
  • How we use and store it
  • Your rights under UK GDPR
  • How cookies are used on our website

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you contact us or use our website:

  • Name and contact details (email, phone number)
  • Business information you provide when requesting support
  • Technical information such as IP address and browser type
  • Any information you choose to send via email or contact forms

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide IT services
  • Maintain internal records and project notes
  • Improve our website and services
  • Meet legal or regulatory obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contract: to provide services you request
  • Legitimate interests: to operate and improve our business
  • Consent: when you explicitly provide it (e.g., optional contact forms)

5. How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your data is stored securely using industry‑standard technical and organisational measures. We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing.

6. Sharing Your Information

We may share data only when necessary:

  • With trusted service providers (e.g., email hosting, cloud storage)
  • When required by law or regulation

We never sell personal data.

7. Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies for basic functionality and security. We do not use analytics, tracking, or advertising cookies.

7.1 Types of Cookies We Use

  • Essential cookies: required for the website to function correctly.
  • Security cookies: used to protect the site from malicious activity.

7.2 Third‑Party Cookies

Some pages may embed external content (e.g., LinkedIn, Google Business Profile). These services may set their own cookies. We do not control these cookies.

7.3 Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect website functionality.

8. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to processing
  • Request data portability

To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

If you have questions about this policy or your data, you can contact us at:
