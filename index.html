Founder Story

Atkinson IT Ltd was founded by Bernard Atkinson, an engineer with over 25 years of hands‑on experience across infrastructure, diagnostics, systems thinking, and technical leadership.

Today, that same engineering discipline underpins the consultancy — offering practical, reliable support to organisations that need clarity, stability, and technical confidence.
