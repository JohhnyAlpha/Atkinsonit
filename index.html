Founder Story

Atkinson IT Ltd was founded by Bernard Atkinson, a heritage‑grade systems engineer with nearly four decades of experience across infrastructure, diagnostics, storage, networking, Linux, and enterprise reliability.

Bernard’s engineering discipline — forged through hands‑on work with PRIMOS minis, early enterprise networking, mission‑critical financial systems, and modern virtualisation platforms — underpins the consultancy’s calm, structured approach to solving complex technical problems.
