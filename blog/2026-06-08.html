Weekly SME IT Update

8 June 2026

Home › Blog › Weekly SME IT Update

Microsoft expands secure‑by‑default rollout

Microsoft is enforcing stronger baseline security across more Microsoft 365 tenants, including stricter Conditional Access templates and automatic blocking of legacy authentication. SMEs with older devices or hybrid setups may see more sign‑in prompts or blocked connections.

Google Workspace pricing changes

Google has announced incremental price adjustments across several Workspace tiers. For SMEs with mixed licences, it’s worth reviewing usage — many organisations are paying for storage or features they don’t use.

Major UK phishing campaign targeting SMEs

A widespread phishing campaign is impersonating HMRC, Companies House, and major UK banks. The emails are unusually convincing and often reference real company officers or filing deadlines. SMEs should ensure staff are aware and that MFA is enforced across all critical systems.

Apple issues emergency patches

Apple released urgent patches for iOS, iPadOS, and macOS to fix actively exploited vulnerabilities. If your business uses iPhones or Macs, ensure devices update automatically — many SMEs still rely on manual updates.

Cyber insurers tightening requirements

Insurers are increasingly requiring MFA, privileged access controls, documented patching policies, email filtering, and evidence of incident response planning. SMEs unable to demonstrate these controls are seeing higher premiums — or refusals of cover.
