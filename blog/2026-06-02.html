Security first: ransomware tactics are changing

Attackers are increasingly skipping encryption altogether and going straight for data theft and extortion. That means traditional “we have backups” thinking isn’t enough anymore.

  • Strong identity protection
  • MFA everywhere
  • Reviewing vendor and third‑party access
  • Tightening email security

Cloud costs are quietly rising

Azure, AWS, and Google have all made incremental pricing changes across storage, egress, and GPU workloads. Nothing dramatic individually, but it adds up. A quick cloud cost review can often save SMEs more than they expect.

Microsoft is pushing secure‑by‑default

More services are moving to enforced MFA, improved Conditional Access templates, and automated patching. Good news for security, but it means some organisations will need to tidy up older configurations to avoid disruption.

VPN appliances are becoming a liability

Legacy VPN boxes continue to be a favourite target for attackers. If you’re still relying on one, it’s worth considering a move toward Zero Trust or modern cloud‑based access solutions.

If you want to talk through any of these changes — or you’re wondering what they mean for your business — I’m always happy to help.
