About Atkinson IT

Practical, resilient IT — designed by someone who has already solved the hard problems.

Atkinson IT exists to bring clarity, structure, and engineering discipline to complex technical environments. We help organisations make confident decisions, build reliable systems, and navigate uncertainty with calm, structured leadership grounded in real‑world experience.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: remove unnecessary stress from technology. We design and support systems that are reliable, understandable, and resilient — so your business can focus on its work, not its IT.

Good IT should feel calm, predictable, and almost boring in day‑to‑day use. That’s not an accident — it’s the result of thoughtful architecture and disciplined engineering.

Our Background

Atkinson IT is led by Bernard Atkinson, an engineering‑driven technology leader with more than 25 years of experience in infrastructure, systems engineering, and operational reliability. Bernard spent 8 years as CTO of a major subsidiary of a FTSE‑100 company, overseeing high‑availability systems, global infrastructure, and mission‑critical operations.

His architectural approach was forged in environments where systems were pushed far beyond their limits — and had to stay online. Those experiences shaped a philosophy of resilience, simplicity, and calm engineering that now underpins Atkinson IT.

How We Work

We believe in engineering that is ethical, dependable, and grounded in practical constraints. We emphasise reliability, safety, and long‑term value over quick fixes or unnecessary complexity.

Whether stabilising legacy infrastructure, guiding teams through high‑stakes decisions, or architecting new systems, we bring a structured, systems‑thinking mindset to every engagement.

  • Calm under pressure: we are used to environments where downtime is not an option.
  • Root‑cause focus: we fix underlying problems, not just symptoms.
  • Plain language: we explain options and trade‑offs clearly.
  • Right‑sized solutions: we design for your scale, not for a vendor’s sales target.

Why Choose Atkinson IT

Many IT providers focus on tools and tickets. Atkinson IT focuses on architecture and resilience — the things that determine whether your systems quietly support your business or regularly get in its way.

A Different Kind of Engineering Mindset

Bernard often says he is “lazy” and wants an easy life — in practice, that means designing systems that do not need constant firefighting. If something is painful or fragile, the goal is to redesign it so it becomes simple, robust, and low‑maintenance.

Experience That Shapes Better Decisions

Bernard’s architectural approach was shaped by real‑world events where systems had to remain reliable under pressure. Those experiences taught him how to simplify complexity, anticipate failure modes, and design systems that evolve gracefully rather than break unexpectedly.
