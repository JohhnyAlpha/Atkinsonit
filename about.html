About the Founder — Bernard Atkinson

Atkinson IT is led by Bernard Atkinson, a heritage‑grade systems engineer whose career spans nearly four decades of hands‑on computing — from 8‑bit home machines to global enterprise infrastructure.

Beginning in 1985, Bernard entered IT at a time when systems demanded real engineering, deep understanding, and the ability to work directly with hardware, operating systems, and proprietary architectures.

Bernard’s foundations began with the Commodore 64 and Amiga, learning computing at the metal — timing, memory, registers, and optimisation. This early discipline shaped a career defined by precision, clarity, and deep systems understanding.

Prime Computers — Engineering from First Principles

Bernard’s first professional break came as a YTS engineer at Prime Computers in 1987, supporting PRIMOS, minicomputer architectures, and hybrid PC/greenscreen estates.

Enterprise Infrastructure — Citibank, LDDC, Diners Club

In 1989, Bernard joined Citibank, supporting early enterprise PC deployments, DOS/Windows hybrids, and the transition from standalone systems to networked environments.

He later joined the London Docklands Development Corporation (LDDC), supporting a diverse estate of DOS, early Windows, Prime minis, and line‑of‑business systems.

From 1995 to 2000, Bernard worked for Diners Club International across Farnborough and Zurich, supporting mission‑critical financial platforms and cross‑border infrastructure.

Global Engineering — WPP

Bernard played a key role in global infrastructure consolidation across 40+ companies and 4,500+ users, delivering resilient storage, virtualisation, and networking platforms.
