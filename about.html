About Atkinson IT

Our Mission

Atkinson IT exists to bring clarity, structure, and engineering discipline to complex technical environments. We help organisations make confident decisions, build reliable systems, and navigate uncertainty with calm, structured leadership grounded in real‑world experience.

Our Background

Atkinson IT is led by Bernard Atkinson, an engineering‑driven technology leader with more than 25 years of experience in IT infrastructure, systems engineering, and operational reliability. Bernard spent 8 years as CTO of a major subsidiary of a FTSE‑100 company, where he oversaw high‑availability systems, global infrastructure, and mission‑critical operations.

His career spans electrical engineering, legacy systems, cloud transformation, networking, storage, and multi‑disciplinary systems integration. This breadth allows Atkinson IT to bridge the gap between engineering reality and organisational strategy — a perspective few consultancies can offer.

Our Approach

We believe in engineering that is ethical, dependable, and grounded in practical constraints. Our work emphasises reliability, safety, and long‑term value over short‑term fixes or unnecessary complexity.

Whether stabilising legacy infrastructure, guiding teams through high‑stakes decisions, or architecting new systems, we bring a structured, systems‑thinking mindset to every engagement.
