Backup & Disaster Recovery

Protect your business from data loss, hardware failure and unexpected outages.

What this service includes

  • Local and cloud backup design and setup
  • Automated backup scheduling
  • Recovery testing and verification
  • Business continuity planning
  • Secure storage configuration

Who this is for

Any business that relies on critical data, applications or systems to operate.

Problems this solves

  • Permanent data loss after incidents
  • Ransomware impact with no recovery path
  • Hardware failure with no backups
  • Accidental deletion of important files

How the service works

  1. Data audit: Identify critical systems, data and recovery objectives.
  2. Backup design: Choose appropriate local and cloud solutions.
  3. Implementation: Configure backups and monitoring.
  4. Recovery testing: Prove that restore works before you need it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should backups be tested?

We recommend quarterly restore tests to ensure your data can be recovered reliably.

Do you support hybrid backup setups?

Yes — we design solutions combining local storage and cloud backup for maximum resilience.

Can you recover data after ransomware?

If backups exist, recovery is usually possible. We ensure you always have a clean restore point.

Do you monitor backup success?

Yes — automated monitoring alerts us to failures so issues can be fixed before they become critical.
