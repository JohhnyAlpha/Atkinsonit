Legacy Hardware & System Support

Repair, stabilise and repurpose older systems that still matter to your business.

What this service includes

  • Diagnostics and fault finding
  • Hardware repair where viable
  • Operating system reinstalls and cleanup
  • Data extraction and migration
  • System repurposing for lighter roles
  • Migration planning to modern platforms

Who this is for

Businesses with older equipment or POS systems that still perform critical functions.

Problems this solves

  • Failing or unstable legacy hardware
  • Unsupported software with no vendor help
  • Slow performance impacting operations
  • No clear path to modern replacements

How the service works

  1. Assessment: Evaluate hardware, software and business impact.
  2. Repair or stabilisation: Get systems back to a reliable state.
  3. Repurposing or migration: Decide whether to extend life or move on.
