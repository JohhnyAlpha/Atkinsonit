Managed IT service packages

Three clear tiers so you can choose the level of coverage that fits your business today, with room to grow.

Tier 1 — Essential Monitoring

  • 24/7 monitoring of computers and servers
  • Hardware health checks (disk, CPU, RAM)
  • Alerts for key services and performance issues
  • Automated weekly maintenance tasks
  • Monthly health report in plain English
  • Optional backup and security add‑ons

Best for: Smaller businesses that want peace of mind without a full support contract.

Tier 2 — Managed Workstation & Server Care Most popular

  • Everything in Essential Monitoring
  • Full patch management (Windows and key applications)
  • Automated remediation for common issues
  • Remote support for your users and systems
  • Weekly maintenance and optimisation tasks
  • Monthly security and compliance reporting
  • Software inventory and licence visibility

Best for: Growing businesses that want predictable IT and fewer interruptions.

Tier 3 — Fully Managed IT & Security

  • Everything in Managed Workstation & Server Care
  • Network monitoring (switches, firewalls, Wi‑Fi)
  • Backup monitoring and daily verification
  • Security hardening and policy enforcement
  • Quarterly IT strategy and roadmap reviews
  • Priority response SLAs
  • Unlimited remote support, with optional on‑site visits

Best for: Organisations that rely heavily on IT and want a proactive, security‑focused partner.

How we work with you

  1. Assess: Review your current IT estate, identify risks and understand how your business uses technology.
  2. Stabilise: Resolve immediate issues, apply security baselines and bring systems up to an agreed standard.
  3. Protect: Deploy monitoring, patching, backup and security controls appropriate to your chosen service tier.
  4. Optimise: Automate routine tasks, reduce noise and improve reliability and performance over time.
  5. Support: Provide ongoing remote support, regular reporting and periodic reviews to keep IT aligned with your plans.
